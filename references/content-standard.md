# Content and translation standard

## Fidelity

- Preserve the paper's argument and source order. Do not reorganize the article into a teaching outline.
- Maintain a one-to-one paragraph pairing: one English source paragraph followed by one Chinese translation. Do not merge, split, summarize, or silently omit paragraphs.
- Repair only PDF artifacts such as page headers, line wraps, soft hyphens, and words broken across lines.
- Preserve gene and protein symbols, constructs, assay names, abbreviations, equations, statistical notation, sample sizes, doses, time points, citations, and qualifying language.
- Translate technical terms accurately but retain the English term on first mention when a Chinese rendering could be ambiguous.
- Do not make claims stronger or more causal than the source. Preserve hedges such as “may,” “suggest,” “consistent with,” and “we speculate.”

## Section reading notes

Add exactly one short Chinese note after each original section or subsection heading. It should explain the role of that section in the paper's argument, the question being tested, or the evidential transition. Keep it to one or two sentences. Do not add separate concept cards, reviewer questions, project connections, or panel-by-panel interpretations unless the user asks.

## Figures and legends

- Use the complete display figure as published; never crop it into panels for the reader.
- Place each figure at the same logical point as the source, normally after the paragraph that first introduces its result.
- Include the complete English legend and a faithful Chinese translation immediately below the figure.
- Preserve panel labels, conditions, sample sizes, statistical tests, exact P values, error-bar definitions, replicate statements, and scale bars.
- Keep legends visually secondary and compact. Do not replace the legend with an explanation.

## Default scope

Include the article's main reading narrative: metadata, Abstract, Introduction/background, Results and all Results subsections, and Discussion/conclusion. Include all main display figures and legends. Methods, supplementary material, References, acknowledgements, author contributions, competing interests, and data/code availability are opt-in unless the user explicitly requests the complete PDF.
